# Post-Purchase, Email 2 of 3: Protect the First Result
**Trigger:** First order placed. Email 1 is the confirmation and routine card. This email sends on day 10. **Job:** prevent the two mistakes that kill results and generate refunds.

**Subject:** Day 10: the two mistakes to skip
**Preheader:** More is not faster. Here is your week 2 check-in.

**Body:**
Ten days in, here is what normal looks like: smoother texture, maybe some dryness around the nose and chin. That is the serum working at starter pace.

The two mistakes we see at exactly this point:

**Going nightly too fast.** Stay at three nights a week until week 3, even if your skin feels fine. The irritation that makes people quit usually arrives at week 3, from enthusiasm at week 1.

**Adding a new exfoliant.** Your routine is serum plus what you already own. New actives this month make it impossible to know what is doing what, and they are the top cause of the "it burned me" reviews in this category.

Week 6 is your next checkpoint. We will check in then, and that is when the concern you named should start to move.
