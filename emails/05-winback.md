# Winback, Email 1 of 3: One Honest Last Case
**Trigger:** 90 days since last purchase, not subscribed. **Job:** name the likely reason she left and answer it once. After this flow, she is suppressed. Sending forever is not a strategy.

**Subject:** We probably got the interval wrong
**Preheader:** The most common reason people leave, and the fix.

**Body:**
It has been about three months, so let us guess honestly about why.

Most people who stop do not stop because the serum failed. They stop because a bottle arrived while the last one was still half full, and a subscription that piles up feels like a bill, not a routine.

If that was you, the fix is boring and it works: 60 or 90 days instead of 45, set by how fast you actually use it. Same serum, same price per bottle, timed to your bathroom shelf instead of our shipping calendar.

If it was something else, the reply button works. A person reads it, and the answer shapes what we fix next.

**CTA:** Restart on a 60 or 90-day rhythm
**Secondary:** Or tell us what went wrong (reply)

*Flow note: email 2 (day 7) is a short plain-text note from the founder persona asking one question. Email 3 (day 14) closes the loop and confirms suppression, which protects deliverability for the engaged list. Winback success is measured on reactivation within 30 days, and on what the replies teach, not just revenue.*
