# Email 1 of the Welcome Flow: The Routine, Not the Discount
**Trigger:** Quiz completed or email signup. **Send:** immediately. **Job:** set the 90-day expectation and teach the routine. No offer in this email, on purpose.

**Subject:** Your skin does not need more products. It needs 90 days.
**Preheader:** Here is the routine your quiz answers point to.

**Body:**
You answered three questions before you gave us your email, so let us use them.

Your main concern is [quiz_concern]. Your skin leans [quiz_skin_type]. You are currently using [quiz_current_routine]. That combination points to one routine, not a shelf of products:

**Night:** cleanse, then Renewal Night Serum, then moisturizer if your skin feels dry.
**Morning:** rinse, moisturizer, SPF.

That is it. The serum is the only step doing new work, which is how you will know whether it works.

Here is the honest timeline. Week 2: texture feels smoother, nothing dramatic. Week 6: the concern you named starts to shift. Week 12: that is when you judge us. Anyone who promises faster is selling you something.

**CTA:** See how the serum fits your routine
