Subscription Economics Calculator

A planning model from Lifecycle in a Box (Amy Kniss). Invented defaults from a fictional brand. Change the interval and watch what a too-short interval does to a year of subscription revenue: it does not create more orders, it creates earlier cancellations.

Model: if the delivery interval is shorter than the true usage interval, the extra cancellation rate applies every month (inventory pile-up). Revenue is summed over 12 months of a decaying subscriber base. This is a teaching model, not a forecast.
