# Data Dictionary (all synthetic)

**customers.csv**: customer_id, signup_date, concern (quiz answer 1), channel (acquisition source), quiz_completed (yes/no).
**orders.csv**: order_id, customer_id, order_date, product, order_type (one-time, subscription, subscription renewal), revenue (USD, invented price points from the brand brief).

Generated by `generate_data.py`, seed 42, signup window 1 Jan to 29 Jun 2026, subscription renewals projected 90 days past the window so cohort behaviour can be read. Regenerate any time: `python3 data/generate_data.py`.
