# Brand Brief: Solmere Botanics (fictional)

## The brand
Solmere Botanics is an invented DTC skincare brand. It sells one hero product well instead of forty products thinly, which makes it a clean teaching model for subscription lifecycle.

## Product and pricing (invented)
- Hero product: Renewal Night Serum, 30ml. One-time $68. Subscribe and Save $54.40 (20% off).
- Supporting products: Gentle Cream Cleanser $34, Daily Mineral SPF $38.
- A 30ml serum used nightly runs out in about 45 days. That single fact drives the subscription design: the default interval is 45 days, not 30. A 30-day default ships product faster than customers use it, and excess inventory at home is the top driver of subscription cancellation in this category.
- Shipping: free over $50, which the subscription price clears on its own.

## Customer (invented persona, one only)
Women 35 to 55, buying for a named concern (dark spots, fine lines, or sensitivity), already spending on skincare, skeptical of claims because she has been disappointed before. She does not need a discount to try. She needs a reason to believe this time is different, and a routine she can actually follow.

## Unit economics (assumptions, stated so the strategy can be judged)
- Gross margin on the serum: 74% one-time, 66% on subscription pricing.
- Blended CAC assumption: $41. First-order contribution is roughly breakeven by design, so the business only works if repeat and subscription revenue land. That is deliberate: it is the situation lifecycle is hired to fix.
- Target: subscription share of orders above 35% by month six, second-order rate above 40% by day 90.

## Voice
Plain, specific, no miracle language. Every claim is tied to the routine and the timeline: what to expect at week 2, week 6, week 12. Credibility is the conversion strategy.
